Understanding the Bottleneck

Why Normal 1-on-1 Swapping Fails

College students have tons of skills to teach, but 1-to-1 trades almost always get stuck. Here is why standard exchanges fail, and how our 3-way system fixes it.

🎤 Presentation Point (The Problem): "Explain the 1-on-1 deadlock to your evaluator: 'Imagine Student A knows Python and wants to learn Spanish. Student B knows Spanish, but wants to learn 3D Animation—not Python! In a simple 1-on-1 world, they are completely blocked. Statistically, less than 4% of students match directly. That's why our project is needed.'"

1. The 1-on-1 Trade Block

For a direct trade, Student A must teach what Student B wants, AND Student B must teach what Student A wants. On a real campus, this mutual match happens less than 4% of the time.

85%+ Trades Get Blocked

2. Expensive Learning Barriers

Private coaching and external tutors are way too expensive for many students, and not everyone can afford them. Peer exchange unlocks all this knowledge freely through mutual student collaboration.

Zero Financial Barrier

3. Trust & No-Shows

Informal skill trades fail when someone doesn't show up. Our platform uses verified student badges, reputation ratings, and lesson escrow.

Escrow Guarantee

How the Platform Solves the Problem

Instead of relying on rare coincidence where two students happen to want each other's exact skills, the platform connects students using 3-Way Cycle Matching and an asynchronous Time-Bank Credit System.

If Rai teaches Raj, and Raj teaches Rahul, Rahul completes the loop by teaching Rai. All three students receive their desired skill without any currency, middleman fees, or 1-on-1 barter roadblocks.

100% Peer-to-Peer Automatic Match Discovery Escrow Protection
Platform Tri-Layer Resolution Strategy:
  • Layer 1: Direct 1-on-1 Swap

    Instant pairing when two students want each other's offered skills.

  • Layer 2: 3-Way Cycle Swap

    Connects 3 students in a closed loop (Student A → Student B → Student C → Student A).

  • Layer 3: Time-Banking Credits

    Teach today to earn 1.0 SkillCredit, then redeem it with any peer tutor whenever you want.
